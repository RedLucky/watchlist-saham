# Bloomberg DTRP & DVD: Dividend Trap & Cashflow Run-Rate Analyzer

## 1. Overview & Institutional Rationale

A frequent pitfall for retail investors in the Indonesian Stock Exchange (IDX) is chasing double-digit dividend yields (e.g. 12% – 18%) without analyzing dividend coverage and capital preservation. On the Ex-Dividend Date, high-yield cyclical stocks often plunge by an amount greater than the dividend received ("Ex-Date Drop Trap"), trapping investors in steep capital losses.

The **Bloomberg DTRP (`DTRP <GO>`) & DVD (`DVD <GO>`)** modules evaluate whether an emiten's dividend is safe and sustainable through multi-dimensional fundamentals:
1. **Free Cash Flow (FCF) Coverage**: Verifying whether dividends are funded from genuine cash generated by operations, or merely accounting net profit, legacy retained earnings, or debt.
2. **Payout Ratio (DPR) Guardrails**: Penalizing payout ratios above 85% or 100%.
3. **Dividend Aristocrat Consistency**: Rewarding continuous unbroken annual dividend distributions (5+ and 10+ consecutive years).
4. **12-Month Cashflow Run-Rate (DVD)**: Providing daily, monthly, and annual passive income projections per lot (100 shares).

## 2. Mathematical Formulation & Scoring Engine

### 2.1 Free Cash Flow Coverage

$$\text{FCF Coverage} = \frac{\text{Free Cash Flow}}{\text{Total Dividends Paid}}$$

- $\text{Coverage} \ge 1.0\times$: Fully covered (+15 pts).
- $\text{FCF} \le 0$: Severe risk penalty (-25 pts). Cash burn warning.

### 2.2 Payout Ratio Evaluation

$$\text{DPR} = \frac{\text{Dividends Per Share}}{\text{Earnings Per Share}} \times 100\%$$

- $\text{DPR} > 100\%$: Extreme penalty (-30 pts). Dividend funded via equity dilution or debt.
- $85\% < \text{DPR} \le 100\%$: High payout penalty (-15 pts). Little retained earnings for reinvestment.
- $30\% \le \text{DPR} \le 70\%$: Optimal safety zone (+15 pts).
- $\text{DPR} < 20\%$: Low payout penalty (-5 pts).

### 2.3 Solvency & Leverage (DER)

- $\text{DER} > 2.5\times$: Debt risk penalty (-15 pts).
- $\text{DER} < 1.0\times$: Healthy balance sheet bonus (+10 pts).

### 2.4 Dividend Streak (Aristocrat Tier)

- $\text{Streak} \ge 10\text{ years}$: BEI Dividend Aristocrat (+20 pts).
- $\text{Streak} \ge 5\text{ years}$: Consistent track record (+10 pts).
- $\text{Streak} \le 1\text{ year} \land \text{Yield} > 8\%$: High-yield windfall risk penalty (-20 pts).

### 2.5 12-Month Passive Income Run-Rate (DVD)

Given Market Price $P$ and Dividend Yield $Y\%$:

$$\text{Annual DPS} = P \times \frac{Y}{100}$$

$$\text{Annual / Lot} = \text{Annual DPS} \times 100$$

$$\text{Monthly / Lot} = \frac{\text{Annual / Lot}}{12}$$

$$\text{Daily / Lot} = \frac{\text{Annual / Lot}}{365}$$
